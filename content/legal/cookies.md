This page describes what this website actually stores in your browser. It matches the site as built, and we update it whenever that changes.

## The short answer

This site does not set any cookies of its own. There is no analytics script, no advertising tag, and no tracking pixel anywhere on it.

## What your browser stores

**A theme choice.** If you switch between light and dark, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. This never leaves your device and we never see it.

**A form submission, only if you send one.** If you fill in and submit the contact form, your message is sent to our form provider, Web3Forms, so it can reach us by email. Nothing is stored in your browser as a result of this beyond what the browser itself keeps for the page you were on.

## Third parties

The only outside service this site talks to is **Web3Forms**, and only at the moment you submit the contact form. It is covered in more detail in our [privacy policy](/privacy). No other third-party script, embed, or tracker loads on this site.

## Your choices

Since this site sets no tracking cookies, there is nothing to opt out of. If you want to clear the saved theme choice, clearing your browser's site data for this domain will do it.

## Changes

If we ever add anything that stores information in your browser, we will update this page and the date above, and describe honestly what was added and why.
